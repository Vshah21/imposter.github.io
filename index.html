How to Play

Simple rules for 3 to 12 players

1 Pass & Play Role Reveal

Pass the phone around. Players tap the card privately to reveal their role. Most players see the secret word (Innocents), while the Impostors see only "YOU ARE THE IMPOSTOR".

2 Discussion & Clues

Follow the randomized speaking order! Each player says one subtle word or phrase related to the secret word. The Impostors must blend in without knowing the word.

3 Vote & Win Conditions

After discussion, players vote on who they think the Impostor is. Innocents win if they eliminate all Impostors. Impostors win if they avoid detection or guess the secret word!
