How to Play

Simple rules for 3+ players

1

Pass the Phone

Each player takes turns looking at the secret card. Everyone receives the same secret word—except the designated Impostor(s).

2

Give Clues

Go in a circle. Each player says ONE word or short phrase describing the secret word. If you are the Impostor, fake it and don't give yourself away!

3

Discuss & Vote

When time is up, discuss who seemed suspicious or gave vague clues. Count down 3, 2, 1 and everyone points to who they think is the Impostor!

4

Winning Conditions

Innocents Win: If the majority votes out the Impostor.
Impostor Wins: If they avoid detection OR correctly guess the secret word when accused!
